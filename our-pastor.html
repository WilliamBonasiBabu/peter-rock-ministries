Our Pastor

Pastor John Babu — Senior Pastor

Pastor John Babu serves the Lord together with his wife, Rachel, and their three children.

Called and trained

  • Theological training: Bachelor of Theology, Manna Bible College
  • Pastoral service: Full-time pastor in Manna churches in Madanapalle and Anantapur
  • Following God’s calling: Started an independent ministry with his wife, Rachel, on Old Bypass Road, Madanapalle

Serving at home

Today three churches serve Madanapalle, Gurramkonda and Kondamaripalli, and in October 2026 our new church at Kondamaripalli was dedicated to the Lord.

Serving abroad

Pastor John Babu also travels to serve the Lord on mission trips in Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, the Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Sri Lanka and other places.

Rachel — Pastor John Babu’s wife

Rachel and Pastor John Babu started this ministry together on Old Bypass Road, Madanapalle. She serves with him and their three children across our three churches.

Family

  • Family and ministry: Serving God together with Rachel and their three children, now across three churches
