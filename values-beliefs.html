Values and Beliefs

The foundation of our faith

The Word of God

The Bible is the inspired Word of God, our guide for faith and daily living.

One God

We believe in one God, eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

Jesus Christ

Jesus is the Son of God, our Lord and Saviour. He died for our sins, rose again on the third day and will come again.

The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit fills and empowers believers, and changes lives from within.

Salvation by Grace

We are saved by God’s grace through faith in Jesus Christ, not by our own works.

The Church

The church is the body of Christ, called to worship, pray, fellowship and serve together.

How we live and serve

Worship

We gather to honour God in spirit and in truth.

Prayer and Fasting

We seek the Lord together every Friday in fasting prayer.

Love and Service

We care for one another and for the people around us.

Life Transformation

We trust God to renew hearts, heal families and change lives.
